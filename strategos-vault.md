# STRATEGOS VAULT — 114 Strategic Patterns

> Knowledge file for the STRATEGOS Claude Project. Each entry compresses a historical win or failure to its transferable hidden pattern.

## Business

**[WIN · 2007] Apple iPhone — Axis Shift** — *Apple vs Nokia/RIM*

- Pattern: Don’t compete on the incumbent’s metric. Introduce a metric they’re structurally unable to optimize for.

**[FAIL · 2000] Kodak — Incentive Paralysis** — *Kodak vs digital*

- Pattern: Your org chart is your strategy. If incentives punish the right move, leadership ‘decisions’ are irrelevant.

**[WIN · 1994] Amazon — Time Horizon Arbitrage** — *Amazon vs retail*

- Pattern: A longer time horizon than your competitor unlocks positions they literally cannot respond to.

**[WIN · 2006] Netflix — Scheduled Self-Destruction** — *Netflix vs Blockbuster*

- Pattern: The company that disrupts you should be you. Schedule your own obsolescence or accept someone else’s.

**[FAIL · 2013] BlackBerry — Best-Customer Trap** — *RIM vs iPhone*

- Pattern: Your best customers will faithfully guide you off a cliff. Watch who is GAINING decision power.

**[WIN · 1980] Canon — Invisible Flank** — *Canon vs Xerox*

- Pattern: Enter where the incumbent’s economics force them to ignore you. Their margin discipline is your cover.

**[FAIL · 1998] Webvan — Early Equals Wrong** — *Webvan vs timing*

- Pattern: Being early is indistinguishable from being wrong. Asset-light probes survive timing errors; asset-heavy bets don’t.

**[WIN · 2008] Airbnb — Regulatory Gap Sprint** — *Airbnb vs hotels*

- Pattern: Move in the gap between possibility and regulation; convert users into a political shield before it closes.

**[WIN · 2016] Jio — Solvency Contest** — *Jio vs Indian telecom*

- Pattern: With a war chest from another business, set price to zero and turn the market into a solvency contest you’ve pre-won.

**[FAIL · 2008] GE — Easy Revenue Poison** — *GE vs itself*

- Pattern: Easy revenue atrophies the muscle that built the company while the scoreboard says you’re winning.

**[WIN · 1960] Walmart — Geography Nobody Wanted** — *Walmart vs Kmart*

- Pattern: Saturate territory rivals consider beneath them; build logistics dominance there; the ‘real’ market then comes to you.

**[WIN · 2010] Figma — Platform-Shift Ambush** — *Figma vs Adobe*

- Pattern: When a platform shift arrives, the incumbent’s installed base flips from asset to anchor.

**[FAIL · 1985] IBM — Outsourced the Crown Jewels** — *IBM vs Microsoft/Intel*

- Pattern: Outsource a component without mapping where profits will pool. IBM kept the box; others kept the kingdom.

**[WIN · 2003] Tesla — Top-Down Cost Curve** — *Tesla vs autos*

- Pattern: Enter at luxury where margins fund learning; ride the curve down. Both flanks work if rivals guard only one.

**[FAIL · 2019] WeWork — Narrative Leverage Collapse** — *WeWork vs reality*

- Pattern: A valuation narrative is leverage; it amplifies until one disclosure margin-calls the whole story.

**[WIN · 1971] Southwest — Refusal as Moat** — *Southwest vs majors*

- Pattern: Refuse the industry’s defining features. The cost structure born of refusal is unmatchable by anyone who keeps them.

**[FAIL · 2005] Sears — Internal Cannibalism** — *Sears vs itself*

- Pattern: Forcing divisions to compete imports the market’s brutality without its discipline. The org consumes itself.

**[WIN · 1997] Apple — Kill the Portfolio** — *Jobs’s return*

- Pattern: When dying, the bold move is subtraction. Focus is a weapon precisely because struggling rivals can’t afford it.

**[FAIL · 2011] HP TouchPad — 49-Day Surrender** — *HP vs iPad*

- Pattern: Half-commitment to a platform war buys full cost and zero position. Worse than not entering.

**[WIN · 2014] Nadella — Identity Pivot** — *Microsoft vs its past*

- Pattern: Sometimes the blocking enemy is your old identity. Releasing it unlocked a decade of trapped value.

## Military

**[WIN · 1940] Ardennes — Attack the Belief** — *Wehrmacht vs France*

- Pattern: Map what the opponent believes impossible. That list is their real perimeter — and it is unguarded.

**[FAIL · 1812] Napoleon — Decisive Battle Addiction** — *France vs Russia*

- Pattern: Identify the one thing the enemy’s strategy REQUIRES — then refuse to provide it.

**[WIN · -216] Cannae — Weakness as Bait** — *Hannibal vs Rome*

- Pattern: Display a weakness the opponent is trained to exploit. Their doctrine becomes the mechanism of their destruction.

**[WIN · 1968] Tet — The Second Battlefield** — *NVA vs USA*

- Pattern: A tactical sacrifice that shifts the information environment can outvalue any battlefield win.

**[FAIL · 2003] Iraq — Day-After Vacuum** — *US vs Iraq*

- Pattern: Winning the contest and winning the outcome are different problems. The most dangerous moment is the day after victory.

**[WIN · 1973] Suez Crossing — Cheap Tool, Impossible Problem** — *Egypt vs Israel*

- Pattern: A cheap unglamorous tool on the ‘impossible’ problem beats an expensive tool on the expected one.

**[WIN · 1944] Fortitude — Phantom Army** — *Allies vs Germany*

- Pattern: The opponent acts on their MODEL of you. Shaping the model can be cheaper than shaping the battlefield.

**[FAIL · 1941] Pearl Harbor — Perfect Wrong Objective** — *Japan vs USA*

- Pattern: Perfect execution of the wrong objective is worse than clumsy execution of the right one. What does this make my opponent BECOME?

**[WIN · -480] Salamis — Chosen Ground** — *Greece vs Persia*

- Pattern: Against superior mass, choose terrain that nullifies numbers. The strait makes a million fight as a hundred.

**[WIN · 1950] Inchon — The Insane Option** — *MacArthur vs N.Korea*

- Pattern: The option your own staff calls insane is often unguarded for exactly that reason. Their analysts ruled it out too.

**[FAIL · 1916] Verdun — Attrition Mirror** — *Germany vs France*

- Pattern: A plan to bleed the enemy white bleeds you identically. Never adopt a strategy whose metric is mutual destruction.

**[WIN · 1991] Desert Storm Left Hook** — *Coalition vs Iraq*

- Pattern: Fix attention with the obvious threat; deliver the blow where attention isn’t.

**[FAIL · 1979] USSR-Afghanistan — Cost Ratio Trap** — *USSR vs mujahideen*

- Pattern: Compute who pays more per unit of damage before engaging. If it’s you, the engagement IS the defeat.

**[WIN · 1805] Austerlitz — Gifted Weakness** — *Napoleon vs Austria/Russia*

- Pattern: Abandon the dominant position to bait the enemy into stretching; take it back through their stretched center.

**[WIN · 1066] Hastings — Feigned Retreat** — *Normans vs Saxons*

- Pattern: A disciplined fake retreat converts the enemy’s strongest asset into a rout. Discipline weaponized as theater.

**[WIN · 1942] Midway — Intelligence as Multiplier** — *US vs Japan*

- Pattern: Knowing WHERE let an inferior fleet be superior at the only point that mattered.

**[FAIL · 1961] Bay of Pigs — Consensus Hallucination** — *CIA vs Cuba*

- Pattern: Groupthink in a closed circle manufactures certainty. No one was assigned to argue it would fail.

**[WIN · -331] Gaugamela — Kill the Keystone** — *Alexander vs Darius*

- Pattern: Don’t fight the army; collapse the thing holding it together. Aim everything at the keystone.

## Geopolitical

**[WIN · 1972] Nixon-China — Flip the Estranged Ally** — *US vs USSR*

- Pattern: The cheapest way to weaken your adversary is to flip their estranged partner. Scan the coalition for fractures.

**[WIN · 1948] Marshall Plan — Aggressive Generosity** — *US vs USSR*

- Pattern: Strategic generosity is aggression: it buys alignment the opponent must then pay to break.

**[FAIL · 1919] Versailles — Humiliation Compounds** — *Allies vs Germany*

- Pattern: Victory that humiliates the loser is a loan against the future at ruinous interest.

**[WIN · 2013] Belt & Road — The Unwatched Board** — *China vs West*

- Pattern: While the opponent watches the board you’re famous for, build position on a board they don’t consider strategic.

**[WIN · 1962] Cuban Missiles — Private Off-Ramp** — *Kennedy vs Khrushchev*

- Pattern: Let your opponent retreat with a private win. The public and real scoreboards needn’t match — exploit the gap.

**[FAIL · 1938] Munich — Appetite Feeding** — *UK vs Hitler*

- Pattern: Concessions to an opponent who wants the process of taking fund the next demand, not peace.

**[WIN · 1815] Congress of Vienna — Include the Loser** — *Powers vs France*

- Pattern: Bringing the defeated INTO the order as a stakeholder bought a century of peace. Compare Versailles.

**[FAIL · 1956] Suez — Power Audit Failure** — *UK/France vs reality*

- Pattern: Acting on yesterday’s power inventory. Britain moved like an empire and found it had become a client.

**[WIN · 1990] German Reunification — One-Window Sprint** — *Kohl vs history*

- Pattern: Historic windows open for months, not years. Speed beat consensus before the USSR could reconsolidate.

**[FAIL · 2014] Crimea — Tactical Win, Strategic Bleed** — *Russia vs West*

- Pattern: A quick seizure that triggers permanent coalition and consolidates the target’s nationhood turns one win into compounding loss.

**[WIN · 1979] Camp David — Trade Asymmetric Goods** — *Carter/Begin/Sadat*

- Pattern: Durable deals trade things each side values differently: land for recognition.

**[WIN · 2020] Abraham Accords — Strand the Veto** — *UAE/Israel*

- Pattern: When a stakeholder uses their veto as an asset, make deals that strand it. Bypass turns blocker into bystander.

## Investment

**[WIN · 2007] Big Short — Incentive Blindness** — *Paulson/Burry vs housing*

- Pattern: The largest mispricings hide behind beliefs the consensus is PAID to hold. Look where incentives forbid looking.

**[FAIL · 1998] LTCM — Correlation to One** — *LTCM vs crisis*

- Pattern: Diversification on historical correlation evaporates when needed. Leverage converts model error into extinction.

**[WIN · 1992] Soros vs BoE — The Forced Defense** — *Soros vs Bank of England*

- Pattern: Hunt opponents locked into defending the indefensible by politics or pride. Their obligation is your asymmetry.

**[WIN · 1965] Buffett — Structure Is Strategy** — *Berkshire vs market*

- Pattern: Design your capital structure so you can’t be forced to do the dumb thing at the worst time.

**[FAIL · 2021] Archegos — Self-Blinding Opacity** — *Hwang vs banks*

- Pattern: Opacity that hides your position also hides your risk from yourself. What no one can see, no one can save.

**[WIN · 2013] Icahn-Herbalife — Attack the Public Clock** — *Icahn vs Ackman*

- Pattern: An opponent who announces their trade hands you their pain points and their timeline.

**[FAIL · 1720] South Sea Bubble — Smart Money Trap** — *Newton*

- Pattern: Re-entering a mania you correctly exited is the costliest trade. Genius doesn’t immunize against FOMO.

**[WIN · 1980] Volcker — Credibility Shock** — *Fed vs inflation*

- Pattern: The cure for an expectations problem is overwhelming pain-accepting force. Half-measures extend the disease.

**[FAIL · 2008] Lehman — Liquidity Is Confidence** — *Banks vs run*

- Pattern: You die at the speed of others’ doubt, not your own balance sheet. Overnight funding is a confidence bet.

**[WIN · 2009] Buffett’s Crisis Terms — Cash as Option** — *Berkshire vs panic*

- Pattern: Cash in a panic is an option on every asset at terms you dictate.

**[FAIL · 2000] Dot-com ‘Eyeballs’ — Metric Substitution** — *Market vs gravity*

- Pattern: When an industry invents new metrics to justify prices, the new metric is the bubble’s signature.

**[WIN · 1949] Graham — Margin of Safety** — *Graham vs uncertainty*

- Pattern: You needn’t predict the future if your entry price already assumes a bad one. Buy errors, not forecasts.

**[FAIL · 2022] FTX — Counterparty Halo** — *FTX vs depositors*

- Pattern: Genius reputations suspend due diligence. The brighter the halo, the fewer people check the books.

**[WIN · 1973] Templeton — Maximum Pessimism** — *Templeton vs sentiment*

- Pattern: The price of an asset everyone is forced to sell includes their forced selling, not its value.

## Product

**[WIN · 2004] Facebook — Density First** — *Facebook vs MySpace*

- Pattern: Sequence for density, not reach. Networks compound from full ponds, not thin oceans.

**[FAIL · 2011] Google+ — Ordered Network** — *Google vs Facebook*

- Pattern: Distribution forces adoption but never desire. Copying features without context copies the shell, not the organism.

**[WIN · 2009] WhatsApp — Strategic Refusal** — *WhatsApp vs carriers*

- Pattern: Every feature you refuse is a battlefield you decline. Focus creates unit economics bloated rivals can’t follow.

**[WIN · 2012] Slack — Gatekeeper Bypass** — *Slack vs enterprise sales*

- Pattern: When the gatekeeper is fortified, route through constituents. Arrive at the negotiation having already won.

**[FAIL · 2014] Fire Phone — Strategy Leak** — *Amazon vs users*

- Pattern: A product built for YOUR strategy rather than the user’s problem broadcasts its insincerity.

**[WIN · 2016] TikTok — Shared Assumption Kill** — *TikTok vs Instagram*

- Pattern: Find the assumption ALL incumbents share. It’s the industry’s single point of failure.

**[FAIL · 2020] Quibi — Adjacent Genius Fallacy** — *Katzenberg vs mobile*

- Pattern: Excellence in an adjacent domain transfers worse than anyone believes. Money only delays the news of absent fit.

**[WIN · 2008] GitHub — Become the Verb** — *GitHub vs SourceForge*

- Pattern: When portfolios live on you, switching costs become identity costs.

**[WIN · 1998] Google — Less Is the Feature** — *Google vs portals*

- Pattern: When every rival adds, subtraction is positioning. The empty page declared: we are an answer, not a portal.

**[WIN · 2010] Instagram — Constraint as Identity** — *Instagram vs cameras*

- Pattern: Constraints created a recognizable aesthetic that WAS the brand. Limits are legible; options are mush.

**[WIN · 2021] Wordle — Scarcity in Abundance** — *Wordle vs feeds*

- Pattern: One puzzle a day in an infinite-content world created synchronized culture. The share grid did the rest.

## Competitive

**[WIN · 1995] Intel — Tempo Ownership** — *Intel vs AMD*

- Pattern: Set the tempo of obsolescence and rivals always solve last quarter’s problem. Tempo beats position.

**[WIN · 1975] Toyota — Protected by Mockery** — *Toyota vs Detroit*

- Pattern: Being underestimated is an asset. Never correct an opponent busy explaining why you don’t matter.

**[FAIL · 2007] Ballmer — Obsolete Scorecard** — *Microsoft vs iPhone*

- Pattern: Incumbents grade disruptions with metrics the disruption makes obsolete. Audit your scorecard first.

**[WIN · 1985] New Coke Reversal — Loud Retreat** — *Coke vs Pepsi*

- Pattern: A reversible public failure can generate loyalty no success could. The loud retreat can outvalue the advance.

**[WIN · 2000] Netflix No-Late-Fees — Profit Center Strike** — *Netflix vs Blockbuster*

- Pattern: Attack the revenue your opponent loves and customers hate. They’ll choose margin over survival too long.

**[WIN · 1962] Avis ‘We Try Harder’ — Weaponized #2** — *Avis vs Hertz*

- Pattern: Admitting inferiority converts it into trust and makes the leader’s size the villain. #2 is a stance, not a rank.

**[WIN · 2005] Android Giveaway — Burn the Adjacent Market** — *Google vs Apple*

- Pattern: Give away the layer your rival monetizes to protect the layer you monetize.

**[FAIL · 1996] Netscape — Distribution Beats Product** — *Netscape vs Microsoft*

- Pattern: A 10x product loses to an equal product with 100x distribution. If the rival owns the channel, the war’s over.

**[WIN · 2019] AMD Chiplets — Change the Question** — *AMD vs Intel*

- Pattern: When you can’t win the old question, change it. Intel’s fab pride became a constraint AMD didn’t share.

**[FAIL · 2011] Borders — Outsourced Its Future** — *Borders vs Amazon*

- Pattern: Never let your rival run the channel your future depends on. Convenience today, decapitation tomorrow.

**[WIN · 2017] Patagonia ‘Don’t Buy This Jacket’** — *Patagonia vs fast fashion*

- Pattern: A sincere anti-commercial stance is uncopyable by rivals whose economics forbid sincerity. Values cost something.

## Political

**[WIN · 1932] FDR — Crisis as Mandate** — *FDR vs Depression*

- Pattern: A crisis suspends normal veto players. Emergencies create a window where the impossible is briefly routine.

**[FAIL · 1990] Thatcher Poll Tax — Concentrated Rage** — *Thatcher vs public*

- Pattern: Universal visible pain with diffuse benefits manufactures a coalition of everyone against you.

**[WIN · 1964] LBJ — The Treatment** — *Johnson vs Senate*

- Pattern: Power is inventory: favors owed, fears held, vanities known. Map individuals, not blocs.

**[WIN · 1860] Lincoln — Team of Rivals** — *Lincoln vs faction*

- Pattern: Absorbing rivals into your cabinet converts their ambition into your administration.

**[FAIL · 1968] LBJ Vietnam — Sunk Cost Presidency** — *Johnson vs quagmire*

- Pattern: Each escalation was rational; the sequence was suicide. Audit the SEQUENCE, not the step.

**[WIN · 1994] Mandela — Rugby Reconciliation** — *Mandela vs division*

- Pattern: Adopt the oppressor’s symbol and you take their identity hostage with love. Disarmament through embrace.

**[FAIL · 2016] Brexit Remain — Fear vs Identity** — *Remain vs Leave*

- Pattern: Economic-fear campaigns lose to identity campaigns. Price arguments don’t touch identity votes.

**[WIN · 1980] Reagan — Question as Frame** — *Reagan vs Carter*

- Pattern: Whoever sets the QUESTION wins before answers begin. Frame ownership precedes argument.

## Legal

**[WIN · 1954] NAACP — Precedent Ladder** — *Marshall vs segregation*

- Pattern: Don’t sue for the endgame first. Build small precedents, each making the next inevitable.

**[FAIL · 2001] Microsoft Antitrust — Contempt for the Forum** — *Microsoft vs DOJ*

- Pattern: Contempt for the tribunal converts a winnable case into a character trial. The forum’s respect is oxygen.

**[WIN · 2012] Patent Troll Defense — Collective Spine** — *NewEgg vs trolls*

- Pattern: Against an extractor pricing settlements below litigation cost, one defendant fighting to the end rewrites the math.

**[WIN · 1215] Magna Carta — Codify in Their Weakness** — *Barons vs King John*

- Pattern: Codify constraints during the strongman’s weak moment; the document outlives the moment and binds successors.

- Pattern: Pair a moral narrative with an economic chokehold. The boycott priced injustice daily.

**[FAIL · 2011] Occupy — Energy Without Architecture** — *Occupy vs structure*

- Pattern: Leaderless purity prevents co-option and also prevents winning. Energy without decision architecture evaporates.

**[WIN · 1989] Velvet Revolution — Ridicule the Fear** — *Havel vs regime*

- Pattern: Authoritarian power runs on performed fear. Once fear is funny, it’s finished.

**[WIN · 2014] Ice Bucket archetype — Participation as Status** — *Movements vs apathy*

- Pattern: Make joining visible, fun, and nominating; the cause spreads as social currency, not obligation.

## Cultural

**[WIN · 1962] Beatles in America — Arrive as an Event** — *Beatles vs US market*

- Pattern: Withhold until demand is orchestrated. Entry as cultural EVENT, not product launch.

**[FAIL · 1992] Euro Disney — Imported Assumptions** — *Disney vs French culture*

- Pattern: Transplanting a model without renegotiating its cultural assumptions reads as occupation, not entertainment.

**[WIN · 2013] K-pop — Culture as Export Industry** — *Korea vs global pop*

- Pattern: Treat cultural production as industrial policy. Soft power compounds like infrastructure.

**[WIN · 1970] Sesame Street — Trojan Curriculum** — *CTW vs TV wasteland*

- Pattern: Package the medicine inside the candy. Meet the audience inside their existing habit.

## Asymmetric

**[WIN · 1930] Salt March — Force the Oppressor’s Hand** — *Gandhi vs Empire*

- Pattern: Choose an act so just that repressing it indicts the repressor. Make every enemy response a losing move.

**[WIN · 1959] Castro’s 12 — Survive as Symbol** — *Castro vs Batista*

- Pattern: An insurgency’s first product is existence. Surviving visibly against odds recruits faster than winning quietly.

**[FAIL · 1967] Che in Bolivia — Imported Revolution** — *Guevara vs terrain*

- Pattern: A model that worked at home fails abroad without local grievance and network. Revolution is not a franchise.

**[WIN · 1973] OPEC Embargo — Cartelize the Chokepoint** — *OPEC vs West*

- Pattern: Fragmented sellers of a critical input have no power; coordinated they own the world’s throat for a season.

**[WIN · 1980] Solidarity — The Union Loophole** — *Walesa vs Party*

- Pattern: Attack the regime through the institution its ideology cannot ban. Use their legitimacy story as your shield.

**[WIN · 1916] Lawrence — Strand, Don’t Storm** — *Lawrence vs Ottomans*

- Pattern: Hit rails, not forts. A fed useless army drains the empire faster than a dead one.

**[WIN · 1986] People Power Manila — Defection Cascade** — *Filipinos vs Marcos*

- Pattern: Regimes fall when enforcers defect. Make the soldier’s choice easier to answer with ‘join.’

**[FAIL · 1971] Weather Underground — Violence Shrinks the Coalition** — *Radicals vs opinion*

- Pattern: Escalating past your sympathizers’ tolerance turns a movement into a cult and gifts the state its justification.
